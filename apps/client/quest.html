CONTROL EXPERIMENT

STRATEGIC · UNIT ICONS

THREE MAIN BATTLES + FOUR DEPTH SKIRMISHES

Depth skirmishes

In headset: touch a ship with your controller or fingertips, then trigger/pinch to select or attack. Trigger in empty space to move there. Hold one pinch to pull the battle; use two pinches to spread, lift and turn the battle horizontally. It stays level. Raise your left hand for controls. Controller grips work too.

Fleet production

Basic yard: fighters, bombers, frigates & artillery. Fleet yard unlocks cruisers; capital yard builds siege capitals and battleships. Add storage to afford them. Costs: alloy / energy.

Base construction

Place near friendly structures. Extractors need a gold deposit. Fleet yard requires a basic shipyard; capital yard requires a fleet yard.
